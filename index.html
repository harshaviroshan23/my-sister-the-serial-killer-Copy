2018 Psychological Thriller 7 Episodes

My Sister, the Serial Killer is a darkly humorous psychological thriller written by Oyinkan Braithwaite. Set in Lagos, Nigeria, the story follows two sisters, Korede and Ayoola, whose lives are connected by a secret that becomes increasingly difficult to hide.
